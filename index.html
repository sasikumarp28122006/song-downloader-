About Tamil Beats

Tamil Beats helps you find and enjoy Tamil music. Every download button works only for tracks that are original, royalty-free or cleared for distribution. For commercial film songs, the card links to the official streaming or store page instead.

Replace the demo music with licensed tracks

Open index.html and edit the SONGS array. Put your audio in an audio/ folder, then set src: "audio/my-song.mp3" and dl: true only if you own or license the right to distribute it. For songs you cannot host, set dl: false and add store: "https://…official link…".
